1. Getting started

Creating an account

Go to cloudtrips.uk/signup and enter your email and a password. After signing up, check your inbox for a verification email and click the link to activate your account. You won't be able to log in until your email is verified.

If the verification email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder. You can also request a new one from the verification page.

Logging in

Go to cloudtrips.uk/login and enter your email and password. If you've forgotten your password, click Forgot password? and follow the reset instructions sent to your email.

Dark and light mode

Click the sun/moon icon in the top-right header to switch between dark and light mode. Your preference is saved automatically.

2. Managing trips

Creating a trip

From the home screen, type a name in the Trip name field and click Add. Your new trip appears in the grid immediately.

Renaming a trip

Click the pencil icon on any trip card to rename it inline. Press Enter or click away to save.

Duplicating a trip

Click the copy icon on a trip card to create an exact duplicate including all activities. Useful as a template for similar trips.

Deleting a trip

Click the bin icon on a trip card. You'll be asked to confirm. Deletion is permanent and removes all activities within the trip.

Searching and filtering

Use the search bar to filter trips by name. Use the year dropdown to show only trips from a specific year based on their activity dates. Trips load 20 at a time — click Load more at the bottom if you have many trips.

Trip notes

Open a trip and click the notes area at the top to add free-text notes — packing lists, reminders, booking references, anything. Notes are saved automatically as you type.

3. Activities

Activities are the building blocks of a trip — flights, drives, hikes, accommodation, city visits, and anything else. Each activity can have a type, name, location, dates, cost, distance, and notes.

Activity types

Type Use for
✈️ Plane Flights, transfers by air
🚗 Car Road trips, drives, car rental
🥾 Hike Walking, hiking, trekking
🏙️ City Sightseeing, city days, tours
🏨 Stay Hotels, Airbnb, camping
📍 Other Anything else

Adding an activity

Inside a trip, click Add activity. Fill in the fields you need — only the name is required. Click Save.

Editing an activity

Click any activity card to expand it for editing. Changes are saved when you click Save.

Reordering activities

Use the up and down arrow buttons on each activity card to change the order within the list.

Filtering and searching activities

Use the search box above the activity list to filter by name or location. Click the type filter buttons (plane, car, hike, etc.) to show only specific types. Activities load 50 at a time — click Load more if you have a long trip.

Dates and times

Each activity has an optional start date/time and end date/time. Dates are used to organise the Timeline view and calculate trip duration in Stats. If no dates are set, the activity appears under "No date" in the timeline.

Distance

You can record a distance in kilometres for any activity. This is totalled in the Stats view and shown in the PDF export.

4. Timeline view

Click Timeline in the trip navigation to see all activities grouped by day in chronological order. The timeline shows start and end times, activity type icons, costs, and locations.

You can expand or collapse individual days by clicking the day header, or use Expand all / Collapse all to toggle everything at once. The timeline is also what gets printed when you export to PDF.

5. Costs & currencies

Each activity can have a cost and a currency. CloudTrips supports any currency — just type the amount and select the currency code (EUR, USD, GBP, CZK, etc.) from the dropdown.

Costs page

Click Costs in the trip navigation for a full breakdown. You'll see total costs per currency, a breakdown by activity type, and a day-by-day cost chart. If your trip uses multiple currencies, each is totalled separately and you can choose a display currency to convert everything using live exchange rates.

Default currency

You can set a default currency in Settings so new activities always pre-fill with your preferred currency.

6. Sharing trips

You can share a read-only view of any trip with anyone — no account required to view. Click the Share button inside a trip to create a share link.

Share modes

Mode What the viewer sees
Full Everything — activities, costs, distances, notes
Public Activities only — costs and distances are hidden

Share links can be revoked at any time from Manage shares. You can also disable all active share links for a trip at once.

7. Collaboration

You can invite other CloudTrips users to collaborate on a trip. Collaborators can add, edit, and delete activities but cannot delete the trip or manage share links — only the trip owner can do those.

Inviting someone

Click the Members button inside a trip, enter the email address of the person you want to invite, and click Send invite. They'll receive an email with an invitation link. The link expires after 48 hours — you can resend if needed.

The person must have a CloudTrips account to accept the invitation.

Removing a collaborator

Open the Members panel and click Remove next to the person's name.

Leaving a trip

If you're a collaborator (not the owner), you can leave a trip from the Members panel.

8. Export & print

Click the Export button inside a trip to access export options.

Download PDF

Generates a clean A4 PDF of your trip itinerary — day-by-day activities, times, costs breakdown, and summary. The PDF is rendered on the server and downloaded directly to your device.

Free accounts include 5 PDF exports per month. The counter resets on the 1st of each month. Your remaining exports are shown next to the Download PDF option.

Export CSV

Downloads all activities as a CSV file. You can open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app. Useful for further analysis or keeping a local backup.

Print

Opens a print-friendly version of the itinerary in your browser. Use your browser's print dialog to print or save as PDF locally.

9. Stats

Click Stats from the home screen to see an overview of all your travel across every trip.

  • Total trips, activities, days, distance, and costs at a glance
  • World map showing countries you've visited
  • Filter stats by year or country
  • Per-trip breakdown table sortable by days, activities, distance, and cost
  • Multi-currency support — choose a display currency to convert all costs

Visited countries

The world map tracks countries you've visited. You can add or remove countries manually using the country selector on the Stats page. This is separate from trip data — adding a country to the map doesn't create a trip, and deleting a trip doesn't remove the country from the map.

10. Settings & account

Click the gear icon in the top-right header to open Settings.

Default currency

Set a default currency for new activities. All new cost fields will pre-fill with this currency.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account. You'll need to enter your password to confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent — all your trips, activities, and personal data are removed. A confirmation email is sent to your address.

If you change your mind, there is no undo. Export any important data before deleting.

11. Installing as an app

CloudTrips can be installed on your device like a native app — no app store required.

On desktop (Chrome / Edge)

Look for the install icon (⊕) in the right side of the address bar, or go to the browser menu and click Install CloudTrips. The app opens in its own window without browser chrome.

On iPhone / iPad

Open cloudtrips.uk in Safari, tap the Share button, then tap Add to Home Screen. The app icon appears on your home screen and opens in full-screen mode.

On Android

Open cloudtrips.uk in Chrome. You'll see an Add to Home Screen banner, or you can tap the menu and select Install app.
